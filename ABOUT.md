# About me

My name is Frederik Handberg. I'm 24 years old and studying Software Engineering in Horsens, Denmark 🇩🇰

I'm passionate about building cool and useful technology 🚀

That ranges from apps that help people think, create, and get things done to autonomous drones that can support security and search-and-rescue teams.

I'm bringing these interests together at [ForwardFuture](https://forwardfuture.world/). The company's mission is to accelerate the world forward to a more sustainable future. Its three brands each have a mission of their own: VikingRobotics, HAPPYDIAMOND, and BeyondBiology.

**HAPPYDIAMOND logo**
**HAPPYDIAMOND** makes high-end apparel and gear built for demanding conditions, with a style you’ll want to wear every day, whether you’re in the city or out in nature.

Durability is a core principle to ensure pieces last for years and can survive tough conditions.

Clothing so good it makes you happy to wear it. That’s the goal.


> My interest in fashion is mainly in techwear and classic suits. I like functional techwear that looks good, works in any environment, and lasts for years. I had trouble finding clothes that fit the way I wanted. That’s what eventually got me into sewing, as I wanted to design and tailor my own clothes to fit me perfectly.


**BeyondBiology logo**
Experimenting with plant technology.

**BeyondBiology** is researching plant fertilizers that can significantly increase growth rates.

Brassinosteroids can potentially play a major role in a sustainable future that allow crops to continue growing and at a much faster rate even in changing conditions due to climate change.


I also work as a freelance news photographer. In my spare time, I'm into motorcycles and cars, and I love animals and spending time in nature.
