Showing the VikingRobotics.tech mission

At VikingRobotics, I’m building advanced drone technology for missions ranging from search and rescue to defense. I design the drones to be modular so they can adapt to what each mission needs.

I design custom frames in CAD, 3D-print the molds, and use them to make carbon fiber frames and structural components. I also develop the software application that operators use to plan flights, monitor their drones, and control them from the ground.

Visit VikingRobotics.tech

HAPPYDIAMOND is where I'm building a HAPPY WORLD.

I’m tired of companies deciding what we’re allowed to fix, when we have to replace the things we buy, and how much of our lives they get to track.

To me, a happy world is one where the things you use every day are fun to use, built to last, easy to repair, and doesn't spy on you. When you buy something, it’s yours to keep, change, and make your own.

At HAPPYDIAMOND, I'm sewing functional techwear and workwear designed to look cool and last for years. I’m also building open-source apps that help you stay organized and get things done without tracking you.

Visit HAPPYDIAMOND.world
